Shivalli · Traditional foods and sweets

Heritage food packs that printed right the first time

Ready-meal cartons and traditional sweet packs that look heritage and modern at the same time.

  • Packaging Design
  • Dieline Design
  • Print Design
  • Food Packaging
  • Label Design
PlatformPackaging Design
What we didPackaging Design, Dieline Design, Print Design, Food Packaging, Label Design
FormatCarton, sweets sleeve

The challenge

A traditional brand moving ready-to-eat meals into retail. Heritage recipes, but packs needed to compete with modern brands. Cartons had to fit the printer’s die exactly.

What we did

  • Khichdi carton on the full flat dieline, with an illustrated story panel
  • Warm, appetising colour blocking that reads from a distance
  • Mysore Pak sleeve with a traditional recipe cue and window

The result

Printed right the first time on the supplier’s die.

  • Stands out next to national brands
  • The pack tells the heritage story, so the shelf does the selling
