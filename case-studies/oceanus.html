Oceanus Study Abroad · Study abroad consultancy

A website and Instagram that turn anxious families into booked consultations

We built Oceanus’s website and run its organic Instagram, so students and parents meet a consultancy they can trust before they ever call.

  • Wix
  • Web design
  • Lead capture
  • Organic Instagram
  • Social post design
PlatformWix + Instagram
What we didWeb design, Wix development and organic Instagram
Live siteoceanusstudyabroad.com

The challenge

Choosing a study abroad consultant is a big, expensive decision for students and parents, and most consultancies look the same online: stock photos, flags and busy flyers. Oceanus needed a website that shows its services, destinations and track record clearly, and an Instagram that backs it up every week.

What we did

  • A website hero built around one action: book a free consultation
  • A six-step service journey, from course choice to life after arrival
  • A university logo strip with a clear disclaimer, building trust without overclaiming
  • Illustrated country cards, colour-coded track record stats, student testimonials and a short enquiry form
  • On Instagram: launch posts that explain every service in one look
  • A destination series with one template per country, so new posts take minutes, not days

The result

One consistent story from the first Instagram post to the consultation form, built to get families to book that first call.
