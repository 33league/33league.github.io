Bangalore Bag Company · Sustainable totes · Bengaluru

Every pop-up stall now brings in new followers and free content

The challenge

Bangalore Bag Company makes sustainable raw cotton tote bags and sells at pop-ups, events and online. Every stall brought sales, but those buyers left without becoming part of the brand online.

What we did

  • Ran a follow-for-a-discount offer at every stall: buyers followed the page and got an instant discount
  • Photographed happy buyers with their totes and turned them into posts
  • Shared customer stories, a brand ambassador post and product details
  • Designed launch creatives, from coming-soon teasers to the pet bag collection
  • Posted timely local moments, like the RCB win, to keep the brand part of the city

The result

Every stall now produces followers and content at the same time.
