Angooti · Experience studio · Bengaluru

From hit-or-miss influencer posts to enquiries every single day

An always-on Click-to-WhatsApp campaign that replaced unpredictable influencer spikes with a steady flow of booking enquiries.

  • Creative testing

Jun to Oct 2026. Figures from the client's ad accounts and sales records.

The challenge

Angooti runs hands-on ring-making workshops where couples, families and teams craft their own gold, silver or platinum rings with a master jeweller. They relied on influencer marketing: some posts brought enquiries, many didn't, and demand rose and fell with each one. They wanted something stable they could plan around.

What we did

  • Set up an always-on Click-to-WhatsApp campaign on a fixed daily budget, sending local enquiries straight to Angooti's team
  • Built the campaign structure for a Bengaluru-only audience
  • Tested static ads against Reels across two audience approaches
  • Moved budget month on month to the audiences and creatives with the lowest cost per conversation

The result

A steady, predictable stream of booking conversations instead of one-off spikes. Angooti's team closes the bookings in chat, so we report results up to the conversation.

  • Cost per conversation down 17% from June to July (₹44.25 to ₹36.60)
  • Cost to reach 1,000 people (CPM) down 54% from June to August
  • The top static ad alone drove 1,450 conversations, half of all chats
  • 19.5 lakh impressions from ₹1.22 lakh of managed ad spend
