One studio, four audiences: Instagram for two games, a brand and an anime

Six months of Instagram content across two games, a studio brand and an original anime, each with its own voice.

  • Organic Instagram
  • Content strategy
  • Reel editing
  • Copywriting
PlatformOrganic Instagram
What we didOrganic Instagram, Content strategy, Reel editing, Copywriting
TimelineAug 2025 to Feb 2026

The challenge

Sutra Digital is an Indian mobile game studio with two games on Google Play (Number Fusion and Find My Pet) and an original anime series on YouTube. Each needed its own audience, from casual puzzle players to anime fans.

What we did

  • Built four content tracks, each with its own tone: Number Fusion, Find My Pet, the studio brand and the anime series
  • Designed posts, carousels and reels, from cute game characters to reels cut from the studio's own anime
  • Wrote captions, on-screen hooks and calls to action pointing to Google Play or a DM
  • Planned festive and brand moments to keep the studio visible between launches
  • Ran a five-part character reveal series ahead of the anime launch, later amplified with Google Ads

The result

A studio feed that sells two games and an anime without them blurring into one another. It also set up the paid campaigns that followed.
