Work/Hunger Nuts
Hunger Nuts · Premium dry fruits · D2C

A premium dry fruit store built to sell gift boxes, not single packs

A full Shopify build and conversion redesign that justifies a premium price and pushes gift boxes and combos.

  • Shopify
  • Conversion optimisation
  • UI/UX design
  • Mobile-first
PlatformShopify
What we didShopify development and conversion design
Live sitehungernuts.com

The challenge

Hunger Nuts sells premium dry fruits in a market full of cheaper, look-alike options on marketplaces. Shoppers needed a reason to trust the brand and pay more, and the store needed to sell gift boxes and combos, not just single packs, especially in the festive season.

What we did

  • Full Shopify build: theme, custom sections, products, payments and apps
  • Conversion redesign: offer marquee, trust bar, discount badges and quick add to cart
  • Gift boxes, combo packs and a build-your-own box to raise order value
  • A seasonal campaign hero ready for festive pushes like Diwali gifting
  • Proof that justifies the price: Google and Amazon ratings, FSSAI and NABL badges and a side-by-side comparison
  • Product pages with weight variants, per-100g pricing and nutrition bars, plus a sourcing map and shoppable videos

The result

A store that gives shoppers a reason to choose Hunger Nuts over cheaper marketplace options, and to add more to the cart when they do.
