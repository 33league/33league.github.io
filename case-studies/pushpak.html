Pushpak by Sutra Digital · Animation · YouTube

1.08 lakh YouTube views for a brand-new anime, at ₹0.07 each

Character-reveal reels built the anticipation, then a YouTube views campaign put an original Indian anime in front of a real audience.

Launched 30 Dec 2025. Figures from the client's ad accounts and sales records.

The challenge

Sutra Digital released an original Indian anime on a brand-new YouTube channel. Without an audience, even strong animation gets no views.

What we did

  • Built anticipation with five character-reveal reels on Instagram
  • Ran a YouTube video views campaign on Google Ads at ₹250 a day

The result

Over one lakh views for ₹7,065, with nearly 40% of people who saw the ad choosing to watch.

  • 2,72,241 impressions
  • ₹25.95 cost per 1,000 impressions
