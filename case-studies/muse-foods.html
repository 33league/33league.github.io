A corporate dining website built to win enterprise clients before the first call

A dark, editorial Framer site that wins the trust of HR and facilities heads and turns visits into corporate enquiries.

The challenge

Muse Foods sells premium corporate dining to large companies. Its buyers are HR, admin and facilities heads who judge a vendor by its website before they ever call. The site had to look as premium as the food and turn those visits into enquiries.

What we did

  • A premium dark editorial design that matches the price point of the service
  • An animated client logo wall, so enterprise buyers see who already trusts Muse
  • Muse's track record in large type: 105M+ meals served a year and 98% client retention
  • Expandable service cards for workplace dining, banqueting and executive hospitality
  • A corporate enquiry form, partner benefits and discovery call booking to capture leads

The result

A website that does the selling before the first call, built with a brand designer and fully responsive.
