Loudmouth · Menswear · India

A menswear store that reads like a fashion magazine

Designed in Figma and built on Shopify, so the handcrafted pieces look premium before the price even shows.

  • Shopify
  • UI/UX design
  • Figma
  • Mobile-first
PlatformShopify + Figma
What we didUI/UX design and Shopify development
Live siteloudmouth.in

The challenge

Loudmouth makes small-batch, handcrafted menswear, known for embroidered trucker jackets and boxy shirts. Standard Shopify themes made the pieces look like any other online store. The brand needed a store that feels like a fashion magazine, so the craft and the “For the Outlier” story come through before the price does.

What we did

  • Designed the full experience in Figma first: homepage, collection, product, cart and mobile
  • Built custom Shopify sections: video hero, marquee, new arrivals grid and editorial story blocks
  • Product pages that answer buying questions: size selector, delivery date estimate, COD badge, wash-care and shipping tabs
  • “You may also like” suggestions and a slide-out cart to raise order value
  • Newsletter capture and social links to bring shoppers back

The result

A store that sells the story as well as the product, built mobile-first across every section.
