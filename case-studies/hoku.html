HOKU! · Olive oil · D2C

An olive oil brand that refuses to look like every other bottle

A bold, playful Framer website that matches HOKU!'s loud voice and stands out from the first scroll.

  • Framer
  • Web design
  • UI/UX design
  • Responsive
PlatformFramer
What we didWeb design and Framer development
Live sitehokuworld.com

The challenge

Olive oil brands in India all look the same: green bottles, quiet labels, health claims. HOKU! is loud and playful on purpose, and it needed a website that felt as bold as the brand.

What we did

  • A site with a personality, not a template: hot pink and green, hand-drawn type and pop art backgrounds
  • Animated, floating product shots
  • Colour-coded variant cards that make the two oils easy to tell apart
  • Illustrated proof points that explain why HOKU! without walls of text
  • Recipes, pairing tips and a customer reviews carousel
  • Sticky pill navigation and a mobile-first responsive layout

The result

A website people remember, in a category where most brands blend in.
