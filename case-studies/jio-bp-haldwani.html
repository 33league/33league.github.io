Jio-bp Fuel Station, Haldwani · Fuel station, Rampur Road, Haldwani

One Hindi message, readable from a moving truck

Launch and diesel offer creatives for a new Jio-bp fuel station in Haldwani.

  • Outdoor Advertising
  • Billboard Design
  • Print Ad Design
  • Hindi Creative
  • Retail Marketing
PlatformOutdoor Advertising
What we didOutdoor Advertising, Billboard Design, Print Ad Design, Hindi Creative, Retail Marketing
FormatHoardings, posters

The challenge

A newly opened fuel outlet that local drivers didn’t know about yet. Truckers pick fuel stops on price and habit. The message had to be read from a moving vehicle.

What we did

  • “Now Open” launch banner for the outlet
  • Hindi offer campaign: diesel ₹1 per litre cheaper than market rate
  • Relatable trucker and driver faces with the outlet in frame
  • Horizontal hoarding and vertical poster formats

The result

One message, cheaper diesel, readable in two seconds.

  • Speaks the local customer’s language
  • Launch and offer look like one campaign across sizes
